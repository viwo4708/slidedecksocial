A presentation game where nobody's seen the slides...

Slide Deck Social is a free online improv/presentation game for 3–8 friends. Everyone uploads six photos from their camera roll and writes two presentation topics. Then each player takes a turn presenting on a random topic while a slidemaster picks the photos and phrases that pop up behind them. Everyone else rates the talk out of 10. Think fast, speak confidently, and get ready to make your case!

  • Free to play online, with no app or account
  • Play in person or over Zoom and Discord
  • Photos are deleted when the game ends
